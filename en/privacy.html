Privacy Policy

ModGarage (the "App") is provided by Ryota Masuda (the "Operator"). This policy explains what information the App handles, where it is sent and when it is deleted.

1. Photos sent to create images

Only when you create an image, the App sends your photo through the Operator's relay server (running on Cloudflare) to the Gemini API of Google LLC (United States). What is sent is the photo after you have blacked out the license plate, and an instruction built from the vehicle type, the modifications you chose and the short note you wrote. A make or model name you type is used only on your device to label your works and is not sent.

The Operator uses the paid Gemini API. Under Google's Gemini API Additional Terms of Service, prompts, photos and responses sent through the paid service are not used to improve Google's products, but Google logs them for a limited period to detect violations of its Prohibited Use Policy.

The relay server does not store photos or instructions. Created images are saved only on your device. The App does not read photo location or date (Exif data).

2. Other information sent

  • RevenueCat, Inc. (US): an anonymous identifier created by the App and your purchase records, to manage purchases. No email or name.
  • Functional Software, Inc. (US, Sentry): when the App fails unexpectedly, event names (opened a screen, chose a photo, started creating), an error context code, device model and OS version. When you tap "Report this image": the reason you chose, the job number, the IDs of the modification options and whether a note was written. Never the photo, the text of your note or the instruction.
  • Apple and Google: purchases are made through the App Store and Google Play.

3. Information kept temporarily by the relay server

The number of images created today per anonymous identifier, and per connection source (a salted hash of the IP address), to prevent abuse. Both are deleted automatically the next day. No ads and no tracking for advertising.

4. Deleting your data

Settings, "Delete all data" removes all works and settings from your device. Deleting the App also removes them. To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us.

5. Age

The App is for people aged 18 and over, as required by the terms of the Gemini API.
