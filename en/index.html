ModGarage Support

ModGarage is an iPhone and Android app that uses AI to show how your car or motorcycle would look with new wheels, body parts or paint. Your photo is sent through the operator's relay server to Google's image generation AI (Gemini API) only when you create an image. The relay server does not store photos, and the images you create stay on your device. The app is for people aged 18 and over. The app screens are currently in Japanese.

How to use

  • Choose or take a photo of a car or motorcycle
  • Black out the license plate by tracing over it (or choose "no plate in the photo")
  • Choose the modifications and tap Create
  • Compare the result with the original, then save or share it

Results are AI-generated previews. They do not guarantee the actual finish, parts fitment or compliance with vehicle safety standards. Images contain Google's invisible SynthID digital watermark.

Reporting an image

Use "Report this image" on the result screen to tell us about an inappropriate image. The image itself is not sent.
